# Marketing and hooks (Phases 0, 2, 3)

The cartoon is the vehicle; the hook, the idea and the single action are the product. Numbers below are working heuristics, not facts: check them against the account's own Insights (retention at 3 s, completion, shares, saves) once a reel has run, and write what you learned into `PLAN.md` for the next one.

## `PLAN.md` template

```
Goal:        <business outcome> -> Action: <ONE thing: comment "WORD" / follow / DM / click / save>
Audience:    <who>, pain in their own words: "<quote-like phrases>"
Core idea:   <one sentence the viewer can repeat to a friend>
Angle:       <myth-bust | before/after | "what nobody tells you" | story | mistake | how-to in 3 steps | ...>
Hook (A/B):  <picked> / <alternate>      (options + scores + which skills generated them below)
Character:   <new design for this video and why it fits the topic>
Palette:     <slot values + one line on why this mood>
Length:      <s>     Voice: <provider/id>     Language: <>
Skills/tools used: <name -> what it contributed>, ...
Learnings:   <filled after posting>
```

## What makes a Reel get watched

| Lever | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hook, 0–2 s | The first frame already moves and already shows the problem, a number, a contradiction or a question. Voice line 1 is the hook itself, not an introduction. |
| Open loop | Promise something the viewer only gets at the end ("and the third one costs nothing"). Pay it off in the last scene. |
| One idea | One message, one audience, one action. If two ideas compete, make two reels. |
| Pacing | New scene every 2–5 s; something moves every ~0.3 s; no dead air. Cut every word that doesn't earn its place — shorter is usually better than longer. |
| Words on screen | The hook also appears as a caption/plaque in the first second (many watch muted). Captions ≤3 words, key word accented. |
| Shareable / saveable | Give the viewer a reason to send it ("send this to someone who...") or keep it (a checklist, a rule of thumb, a surprising number). |
| Ending | Land the payoff, then the CTA in one short line; end on a frame or sound that flows back into the start so the loop invites a replay. |
| Trust | Claims must be true and sourceable. Numbers come from the brand's own data or a verified source; mark estimates as estimates. No invented testimonials or results. |
| Instagram UI | Keep key text out of roughly the top 14 % and bottom 35 % where the platform draws its own UI, except the deliberate caption band. |

## Hook process

The hook is the most important line of the reel and it is **never improvised**. It is produced with the installed marketing, hook, copywriting and social skills, then judged.

1. Write the audience's pain in their words (use research skills/tools, comments, reviews, search data if available).
2. **Run the hook-capable skills** (hook generators, reels/short-video scripting, copywriting, marketing psychology, audience research: use several, in any order) with the pain, the angle and the single action as input. Collect **at least 10 candidates**, across different formulas: bold claim, number, "stop doing X", myth vs truth, curiosity gap, direct address ("you"), confession, contrast, a visual-first moment. The best hooks for this format are *visual* (a counter stuck at 0, a stamp, a bitten coin) with a spoken line that sharpens it. If no hook-capable skill is installed, generate the candidates yourself with this list and say so in `PLAN.md`.
3. **Not a hook** (reject on sight): a greeting, "Hi, today...", "In this video...", introducing the character or the brand by name ("This is Max. He has a website."), a neutral scene-setter, a definition, a question with an obvious dull answer.
4. Score each 1–5 on: stops the scroll in 2 s · clear without sound · specific to the audience · promises payoff · fits the brand tone. Keep the top 3. Run any virality/retention/analysis tool you found on the top candidates (as text or a rough first scene) and note the result.
5. Pick A and B. B ships as the alternate cut (same reel, different first scene) so the user can A/B on the account.
6. Show the options, the scores and which skills produced them at the Phase 3 gate.

## CTA

One action, phrased as an easy micro-step ("Comment WORD and I'll send you ..."), repeated in the caption and pinned comment. Say what they get. If the CTA is a comment keyword, confirm with the user that someone will actually answer it.

## Caption and delivery text

- Caption: first line repeats the hook (it's what shows before "more"), 2–4 short lines of value, CTA line, then a small set of relevant hashtags/keywords for the niche.
- Cover: a hook frame with legible text, composed to survive the profile-grid crop.
- Pinned comment: the CTA plus one extra tip.
- Deliver the alternate hook, the suggested posting notes, and what to watch in Insights.
